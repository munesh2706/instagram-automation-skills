# Instagram Level-10 Hook Writing

## Purpose
Write hooks that:
- **STOP SCROLLERS** (first 0.5 seconds critical)
- **CREATE CURIOSITY GAPS** (make them want answers)
- **AVOID GENERIC PATTERNS** (not "You won't believe...", "Let me show you...")
- **MATCH TOPIC + AUDIENCE** (specific pain points, not generic)
- **DRIVE RETENTION** (make them watch entire video)

## What Makes a Level-10 Hook

### Level 10 ≠ Normal Hooks
❌ Bad: "Let me show you 5 budgeting tips"
❌ Bad: "You won't believe this trick"
❌ Bad: "Watch till the end"

✅ Good: "Your bank account is bleeding money and you don't even know it"
✅ Good: "90% of people fail at budgeting because they're doing this ONE thing wrong"
✅ Good: "The budgeting method that made me save $10k in 6 months (and nobody teaches it)"

## Hook Formula Framework

### Hook Type 1: The Problem Pain Point
**Structure**: [Relatable pain] + [Surprising fact] + [Curiosity gap]
```
"You've been working your ass off for 10 years, 
but you have less money than when you started. 
Here's why..."

"Every time your paycheck hits, 
it's gone by week 2. 
This is the ONE expense killing you."

"You're doing EVERYTHING right with money, 
but you're still broke. 
The real reason is..."
```

**When to use**: Topics about struggles, failures, mistakes
**Emotion**: Validation + frustration + hope

### Hook Type 2: The Surprising Stat/Contradiction
**Structure**: [Counterintuitive fact] + [Contradiction to belief] + [Curiosity]
```
"Rich people don't do what you think they do with money. 
In fact, they do the OPPOSITE."

"90% of people are doing this 'budgeting hack'... 
but it's actually making them POORER."

"The ONE finance rule everyone breaks 
is the ONE that makes you rich."
```

**When to use**: Topics with surprising truths, counterintuitive advice
**Emotion**: Shock + intrigue + "I need to know"

### Hook Type 3: The Pattern Interrupt
**Structure**: [Breaks expected pattern] + [Unexpected angle] + [Must-know info]
```
"Forget everything you know about saving money. 
This is what actually works."

"Your financial advisor is lying to you. 
Here's what they don't want you to know."

"The budget that billionaires use 
(and why you've never heard of it)."
```

**When to use**: Topics challenging conventional wisdom
**Emotion**: Authority challenge + curiosity + excitement

### Hook Type 4: The Specific Example/Result
**Structure**: [Concrete number] + [Timeframe] + [How it's possible]
```
"I saved $15,000 in a year with this ONE budget method. 
Here's exactly how."

"This investing strategy made me $5k per month passively. 
And it's stupidly simple."

"The side hustle that paid my rent in 30 days. 
(No experience needed.)"
```

**When to use**: Topics with measurable results, transformation stories
**Emotion**: Hope + belief + possibility

### Hook Type 5: The Question That Creates Urgency
**Structure**: [Urgent question] + [Stakes] + [Need to answer]
```
"Are you making THIS mistake that costs you $1000/month?"

"What's the one thing billionaires do with money 
that you're NOT doing?"

"If you're not doing THIS, 
are you actually serious about financial freedom?"
```

**When to use**: Topics about opportunity cost, missed potential
**Emotion**: FOMO + urgency + competitiveness

### Hook Type 6: The Controversial/Bold Statement
**Structure**: [Bold claim] + [Opposite of mainstream] + [Proof coming]
```
"Budgeting is ruining your life. 
Here's what to do instead."

"Everything you learned about money is WRONG."

"The 'safe' investment strategy is actually the riskiest."
```

**When to use**: Topics where conventional wisdom is wrong
**Emotion**: Agreement + validation + intrigue
**⚠️ Use carefully**: Must deliver proof. Empty controversy = lost credibility.

## Execution Process

### Step 1: Analyze the Topic
Before writing hooks, understand:
- **Core pain point**: What problem does this solve? (e.g., "Not having enough money saved")
- **Audience emotion**: What do they FEEL? (e.g., "Frustrated, hopeless, overwhelmed")
- **Surprising element**: What's NOT obvious? (e.g., "Most people fail because of ONE habit")
- **The payoff**: What do they GET? (e.g., "$10k saved in 6 months")

### Step 2: Generate Hooks Per Formula
For each topic, write hooks using ALL 6 formulas:
1. Problem Pain Point hook
2. Surprising Stat/Contradiction hook
3. Pattern Interrupt hook
4. Specific Example/Result hook
5. Question That Creates Urgency hook
6. Controversial/Bold Statement hook

### Step 3: Evaluate & Filter
Score each hook on:

| Criteria | Poor (1-3) | Good (4-7) | Excellent (8-10) |
|----------|-----------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Stops Scroll** | Weak, forgettable | Interesting | Impossible to skip |
| **Curiosity Gap** | Obvious, no mystery | Some intrigue | MUST watch to find out |
| **Non-Generic** | Overused phrase | Somewhat fresh | Never heard before |
| **Specific** | Vague, general | Somewhat specific | Concrete + relevant |
| **Audience Match** | Doesn't fit | Somewhat relatable | Hits exact pain point |
| **Deliverable** | Can't prove | Somewhat provable | Clear proof coming |

**Target**: Keep only 8-10 hooks scoring 7+

### Step 4: Test Variations
For top 3 hooks, create 2 variations each:
- **Variation A**: Direct + aggressive tone
- **Variation B**: Conversational + relatable tone

Example:
```
Original: "Your bank account is bleeding money 
and you don't even know it."

Variation A: "STOP: Your bank account is ACTIVELY 
losing money right now. Here's why."

Variation B: "Real talk—your money disappears every month 
and you have NO IDEA where it goes."
```

## Advanced Techniques

### Technique 1: Specificity (Makes it Stick)
❌ Generic: "Budgeting tips that will help"
✅ Specific: "Why 90% of people fail at budgeting (and how to be the 10%)"

Add specific numbers, concrete results, exact timeframes.

### Technique 2: The Loop Hole
Make them think "Wait, what?" → Forces watch
```
"The ONE rule about money everyone breaks... 
is the ONE rule that makes you rich."

"Everyone says to do THIS with money... 
but rich people do the EXACT OPPOSITE."
```

### Technique 3: The Pattern Break
Start exactly like a generic hook, then twist:
```
Expected: "Let me show you 5 ways to save money"
Your twist: "Forget 5 ways. Here's the ONE way rich people actually save."
```

### Technique 4: The Authority Flip
Instead of claiming expertise, challenge it:
```
"Your financial advisor is wrong about this."
"Influencers are lying about how they got rich."
"Everything you learned about investing is backwards."
```

### Technique 5: The Reframing Hook
Flip the narrative:
```
"You're not bad with money. 
You just never learned THIS."

"It's not your fault you're broke. 
The system teaches you THIS instead."
```

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Example | Fix |
|---------|---------|-----|
| Too generic | "Amazing money tip" | "Why 90% fail at budgeting" |
| All hype, no delivery | "This will BLOW YOUR MIND" | Deliver specific, provable info |
| Confusing curiosity with clickbait | "You won't BELIEVE this!" | Create genuine intrigue |
| Ignoring audience pain point | "Cool investing fact..." | "This costs you $1000/month and you don't know" |
| Not specific enough | "Changed my life" | "Saved $15k in 12 months with this method" |
| Breaking promise in video | Hook: "Secret method" → Video: Generic advice | Always deliver what hook promises |

## Hook-to-Script Alignment
Best hooks work because they promise specific delivery:
```
Hook: "The ONE budgeting mistake costing you $1000/month"
Script must: Explain 1 specific mistake + how it costs money + exact solution

Hook: "Rich people do THIS opposite thing with money"
Script must: Show what "normal" people do → Show what rich people do → Explain why
```

## Testing & Optimization
After posting:
- Track which hooks got highest click-through rate
- Note which angles your audience responds to
- Test similar formulas on future videos
- Update hook bank based on performance data

## When to Use Each Hook Type

| Topic Type | Best Hook Formula |
|-----------|------------------|
| Mistakes/failures | Problem Pain Point or Surprising Stat |
| Hacks/methods | Specific Example or Pattern Interrupt |
| Mindset shifts | Controversial Statement or Problem Pain Point |
| Results/transformation | Specific Example or Question |
| Controversial takes | Bold Statement or Surprising Stat |
| Educational | Pattern Interrupt or Question |
| Emotional | Problem Pain Point or Reframing |
