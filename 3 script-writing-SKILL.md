# Instagram ELI5 Script Writing

## Purpose
Write scripts that:
- **ELI5 COMPLEXITY** (explain like to a 5th grader, not expert)
- **INCLUDE REAL EXAMPLES** (2 concrete, relatable examples with numbers)
- **FIT VIDEO LENGTH** (15-60 seconds = 130-200 words)
- **STRUCTURED FLOW** (Hook → Problem → Solution → CTA)
- **VERTICAL FORMAT** (optimized for 9:16 aspect ratio + captions)
- **RETENTION FOCUSED** (keep viewers watching till end)

## Script Structure (Universal Template)

### Section 1: Hook (0-3 seconds)
**Purpose**: Already told in hook. Confirm + expand slightly.
**Length**: 10-20 words

```
"Your bank account is bleeding money and you don't even know it. 
Here's why."
```

### Section 2: Problem Setup (3-8 seconds)
**Purpose**: Paint the picture. Make them relate.
**Length**: 30-50 words
**Technique**: Describe the pain, use "you" language, make it personal

```
"Let's say you make $3000/month. Feels like good money, right? 
But by the end of month, you're broke. 
You don't know where it went."
```

### Section 3: The Culprit/Reveal (8-15 seconds)
**Purpose**: Explain the ONE thing causing the problem
**Length**: 40-60 words
**Technique**: Simple explanation, avoid jargon

```
"It's not because you're bad with money. 
It's because you don't have a SYSTEM. 
Without a system, your money just... disappears. 
You're not tracking it. You're not controlling it."
```

### Section 4: Real Example #1 (15-25 seconds)
**Purpose**: Prove the problem is real with concrete example
**Length**: 50-70 words
**Technique**: Specific numbers, relatable person, specific situation

```
"Take Sarah. She makes $4000/month as a designer. 
She thought she was saving, but when I asked to see her numbers? 
She was spending $400 on subscriptions she forgot about. 
$200 on eating out. $150 on impulse Amazon buys. 
That's $750/month = $9000/year just GONE."
```

### Section 5: The Solution (Simplified) (25-35 seconds)
**Purpose**: Explain the solution clearly
**Length**: 50-70 words
**Technique**: Break into 1-3 simple steps, avoid complexity

```
"Here's what you do: 
STEP 1: Write down everything you spend for one week. 
STEP 2: Put it in categories: Must-Have, Nice-to-Have, Waste. 
STEP 3: Cut the Waste category in half. 
That's it. No complex budget. Just categories."
```

### Section 6: Real Example #2 (35-45 seconds)
**Purpose**: Show the solution working with another relatable example
**Length**: 50-70 words
**Technique**: Different person/situation than Example #1, same solution, big result

```
"My friend Marcus did this. 
He found $800/month in 'waste' category: 
Subscriptions he forgot, eating out, random shopping. 
In 6 months? $4800 saved. 
One year? $9600 saved. 
Same income. Different system."
```

### Section 7: The Big Promise/Result (45-55 seconds)
**Purpose**: Show what's possible if they follow this
**Length**: 30-40 words
**Technique**: Big number, timeframe, emotional payoff

```
"If you do this, you'll save $500-1000/month 
without giving up anything you actually love. 
That's $6000-12000/year. 
Without being miserable."
```

### Section 8: CTA (Call To Action) (55-60 seconds)
**Purpose**: Tell them what to do next
**Length**: 15-25 words
**Technique**: Specific, easy action. Post link or comment to engage.

```
"Drop a 💾 if you're going to track your spending this week. 
Link to the free tracker in my bio."
```

## Word Count Targets by Video Length

| Video Length | Total Words | Reading Speed |
|------------|----------|--------------|
| 15 seconds | 40-60 words | Very fast (3-4 words/sec) |
| 30 seconds | 80-120 words | Normal (2.5-4 words/sec) |
| 45 seconds | 110-160 words | Normal |
| 60 seconds | 130-200 words | Conversational (2-3 words/sec) |

**Rule**: 1 word per 0.4-0.5 seconds for natural speech

## The ELI5 Principle

### How to Explain Complex Topics Simply

**Complex**: "Compound interest is when your interest earns interest"
**ELI5**: "Your money makes babies. And those babies make babies. More babies = more money."

**Complex**: "Dollar-cost averaging reduces volatility in portfolio returns"
**ELI5**: "Instead of buying once, you buy a little bit every week. That way you never buy at the peak. You get better prices on average."

### The Rule: Use Examples > Use Jargon

Replace every complex term:
- "Passive income" → "Money you earn while sleeping"
- "ROI" → "For every $1 you put in, how much do you get back?"
- "Diversification" → "Don't put all eggs in one basket"
- "Liquidity" → "How fast can you turn it into cash?"

## Example #1: Best Practices

### DO:
✅ Use specific numbers (not "a lot", but "$3000")
✅ Use real names or composite examples
✅ Use relatable situations (not billionaires, but normal people)
✅ Show before/after with numbers
✅ One clear solution, not multiple

### DON'T:
❌ Use "approximately" or "roughly"
❌ Use abstract concepts without examples
❌ Use only hypothetical people
❌ Explain 5 different methods (confusing)
❌ Assume they know industry terms

## Example #2: Real Example Structure

**Good Real Example**:
```
"My friend David makes $3500/month. 
He felt broke every month. 
We found: subscriptions ($200), eating out ($300), 
impulse buys ($150) = $650 wasted. 
In 12 months: $7800 saved. 
He didn't cut anything he loved—just the waste."
```

**Why it works**:
- ✅ Specific person (name)
- ✅ Specific income ($3500)
- ✅ Specific categories with numbers
- ✅ Specific outcome with timeline
- ✅ Emotional payoff (didn't suffer)

## Video Format: Text Overlay Considerations

Since Instagram videos are vertical 9:16 with captions:

### What Goes on Screen (Text Overlay):
- **Hook** (4-6 words max): Large text, bottom third
- **Key numbers** (prices, amounts): Highlighted, large
- **Examples**: Name + situation (1-2 lines)
- **Key takeaway**: Center screen, clean font

### What You SPEAK (Voiceover):
- **Full script** with all details
- **Examples** with context
- **Story elements** and emotion
- **CTA** at end

**RULE**: Text should summarize, voiceover should explain

## Script Writing Process

### Step 1: Choose Topic & Hook
Input: Topic + Your hook + Target audience
→ Understand the pain point deeply

### Step 2: Outline 8 Sections
For each section, write:
- Purpose (why is this section here?)
- Core message (1 sentence summary)
- Key words (terms to include)

### Step 3: Write Full Script
Write conversationally, as if you're explaining to a friend.
Don't worry about length yet.

### Step 4: Calculate Reading Time
Count words ÷ 2.5 words per second = time needed

### Step 5: Edit to Fit Length
- Cut filler words ("like", "um", "basically")
- Combine similar points
- Remove redundancy
- Keep real examples (they're essential)

### Step 6: Add Visual Notes
Note where text overlays go, where to pause, where to emphasize.

## Common Script Mistakes

| Mistake | Bad Example | Fix |
|---------|----------|-----|
| Too many solutions | "Here are 7 ways to save..." | "Here's the ONE way that actually works" |
| Vague examples | "My friend saved money" | "My friend David saved $9600/year by cutting waste" |
| No numbers | "Costs a lot" | "$400/month in forgotten subscriptions" |
| Speaking too fast | [Speed reading] | Natural pace, pause at examples |
| Confusing jargon | "Utilize passive income strategy" | "Make money while you sleep" |
| Boring delivery | Robot voice | Conversational, like explaining to friend |
| Too long | 300 words | Trim to 130-200 words |
| No CTA | Script ends, people confused | "Do this → comment → check bio link" |

## Script Variations for A/B Testing

For each topic, write 2 script variations:

**Variation A**: More emotional, story-driven
```
"Here's a real situation. Sarah makes $4000/month. 
Feels like good money until... month ends and she's broke. 
Sound familiar?"
```

**Variation B**: More direct, solution-focused
```
"If you're broke by month-end despite good income, 
here's exactly why it happens and what to do."
```

Test both, see which gets better retention.

## Checklist Before Recording

- [ ] Hook is compelling (not generic)
- [ ] Problem setup is relatable (uses "you" language)
- [ ] Solution is clear + simple (no jargon)
- [ ] Example #1 has specific numbers (not "a lot")
- [ ] Example #2 is different person, same solution
- [ ] CTA is specific (not "subscribe")
- [ ] Total word count fits time (±10%)
- [ ] No filler words ("like", "um", "basically")
- [ ] Can be read naturally in one breath per section
- [ ] Text overlays planned (what goes on screen)
- [ ] Tone matches brand voice
