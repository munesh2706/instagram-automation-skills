# Instagram Deep Research & Topic Selection

## Purpose
Find Instagram topics that are:
1. **Trending NOW** (viral potential in next 2-4 weeks)
2. **Evergreen VALUE** (relevant for months, not just days)
3. **Audience PAIN POINTS** (solves real problems your followers have)
4. **LOW COMPETITION** (opportunity gaps in your niche)
5. **PROOF OF CONCEPT** (already working on other platforms)

## Research Methodology

### Phase 1: Define Your Niche & Audience
Before researching topics, clarify:
- **Niche**: What specific area? (e.g., "personal finance for beginners" not just "finance")
- **Target Audience**: Age, income, problems, aspirations (e.g., "25-35 year olds struggling with debt")
- **Existing Content**: What have YOU posted? What performed well?
- **Competitor Niches**: Who else posts in your space?

### Phase 2: Multi-Source Trend Research

#### A. Google Trends
- Search your niche keywords (e.g., "budgeting", "investing", "side hustle")
- Look for: Rising trends (upward curve) vs declining
- Check: Related queries (what people search WITH your keyword)
- **Find**: 3-5 trending sub-topics

**Action**: Visit `trends.google.com`, search 5-10 keywords, screenshot rising trends

#### B. TikTok & YouTube Shorts Research
- Search your niche hashtags on TikTok (sort by "Latest")
- Check view counts: 100k+ views = proven audience interest
- Analyze: Top comments - what questions are people asking?
- YouTube Shorts: Search same keywords, check highest views
- **Find**: Videos with 500k+ views (these topics WORK)

**Action**: 
```
Search: #[niche keyword] on TikTok
Identify: 10 videos with 500k-10M views
Record: Topic, view count, comment themes
```

#### C. Reddit Research (Deep Gold Mine)
- Subreddits relevant to your niche (r/personalfinance, r/Entrepreneur, etc.)
- **Top posts**: Sort by "Top" (past month/year) - what resonates?
- **Questions**: Sort by "New" - what are people currently struggling with?
- **Comment threads**: What problems keep repeating?
- **Awards**: High-award posts = community validates as valuable
- **Find**: Pain points + trending discussions

**Action**:
```
Subreddit: r/[relevant]
Record top 20 posts from past 30 days:
- Title (the topic)
- Upvotes (proof of interest)
- Top 3 comments (what people care about)
```

#### D. Pinterest Trend Research
- Search your niche keywords
- Check "Tried it" count on pins (proof people want this)
- Look at: Seasonal trends, timing
- **Find**: Topics with 10k+ saves (evergreen demand)

**Action**:
```
Search: [niche keyword] on Pinterest
Identify pins with 10k+ saves
Record: Topic, save count, time period
```

#### E. YouTube Search & Autocomplete
- Type your niche keyword in YouTube search
- Look at: Autocomplete suggestions (what people search for)
- Check video titles of top results: What angles are people using?
- **Find**: Question-based topics (e.g., "How to", "Why", "What if")

**Action**:
```
Search: [niche] on YouTube
Analyze top 20 video titles
Identify: Common question patterns, angles, word patterns
```

#### F. Instagram Hashtag Research
- Search 20 hashtags in your niche
- For each: Check post count (volume) + engagement
- Look at: Top posts (sort by "Most Recent" then manually check engagement)
- **Find**: Hashtags with high engagement but lower post count (less saturated)

**Action**:
```
Research 20 hashtags in your niche:
- #[keyword] - Posts: X, Avg engagement: Y
- Identify: High engagement, lower saturation gaps
```

#### G. Competitor Deep Dive
- Find 5-10 successful accounts in your niche
- Analyze their content:
  - What posts got highest engagement?
  - What topics repeat?
  - What's missing (gaps)?
- **Find**: Topics they DON'T cover (your opportunity)

**Action**:
```
Competitor: @[account]
Top 5 posts by engagement:
1. Topic: [title] | Likes: X | Comments: Y
2. ...
Gaps (not covered): [list topics they avoid]
```

### Phase 3: Viral Factor Analysis

For each topic discovered, score it on these factors:

#### A. Trending Score (1-10)
- Google Trends: Upward curve = 8-10, Flat = 5-7, Declining = 1-3
- TikTok/YouTube: 500k+ views last week = 9-10, 100k-500k = 7-8, <100k = 5
- Reddit: Posted this week + upvoted = 8-10, Posted month ago = 6-7
- **Score**: Average of above

#### B. Pain Point Alignment (1-10)
- Does it solve a real problem your audience has?
- Based on Reddit questions + YouTube comments
- Generic interest = 5, Clear pain point = 9-10

#### C. Competition Level (1-10)
- Lower is better (less saturated)
- High posts on hashtag = high competition (3-5)
- Low posts on hashtag = low competition (8-10)

#### D. Evergreen Value (1-10)
- Will this be relevant in 6 months?
- Time-sensitive trend = 3-5 (viral now, dead later)
- Evergreen + useful = 9-10

#### E. Hook Potential (1-10)
- Can you create a CATCHY hook for this?
- Topics with clear surprises/curiosity gaps = 8-10
- Boring topics = 3-5

#### F. Proof of Concept (1-10)
- Already proven to work on other platforms?
- TikTok/YouTube videos with 1M+ views on topic = 10
- Some proof (100k+ views) = 8
- No proof yet = 5

### Phase 4: Topic Ranking & Output

**Combine all scores**:
```
Topic Score = (Trending×0.25) + (PainPoint×0.25) + (Competition×0.15) + 
              (Evergreen×0.2) + (HookPotential×0.1) + (ProofOfConcept×0.05)
```

**Output Format** (ranked by score, highest first):

```

**Trending**: 9/10 (Google Trends rising 45%, TikTok 2.3M views this month)

**Competition**: 7/10 (12k posts on #[hashtag], but low engagement average)
**Evergreen**: 9/10 (Relevant year-round, no seasonal decline)
**Hook Potential**: 8/10 (Clear curiosity gap: "Why 90% fail at this...")
**Proof**: 10/10 (YouTube videos on topic have 1M+ views)

**Why It Works**: 
- X number of people searching for this monthly
- Specific pain point: [quote from Reddit/comments]
- Example of success: [TikTok video] got 2.3M views

**Content Angles**:
1. "The biggest mistake everyone makes with [topic]"
2. "This [topic] hack changed my [result]"
3. "Nobody talks about the [specific angle] of [topic]"

**Best Time to Post**: [based on when top videos posted]

---

[Same format...]
```

## Important Notes
- **Trending ≠ Evergreen**: Balance both. Topic with score 8.7 includes both.
- **Proof > Speculation**: Only suggest topics that already have 100k+ views somewhere
- **Pain Point > Novelty**: A topic solving a real problem beats a random trend
- **Competition analysis**: Saturation matters—easier to go viral in lower-competition niches
- **Timing**: Post trending topics while they're hot (next 1-4 weeks)
- **Real examples**: Every topic recommendation includes proof (links/data)
