# Instagram Content Calendar Planning

## Purpose
Create calendars that:
- **PREVENT SCRAMBLING** (posts planned weeks ahead)
- **OPTIMAL POSTING TIMES** (when your audience is most active)
- **BALANCED MIX** (70/20/10 rule for content types)
- **BATCH GROUPING** (produce 4 weeks at once, not daily)
- **THEME CONSISTENCY** (diverse but cohesive topics)
- **GROWTH STRATEGY** (mix trending + evergreen + gaps)

## Understanding Your Audience's Activity

### Find Your Peak Times

**Method 1: Instagram Insights (if you have 10k+ followers)**
- Go to Profile → Insights (or Creator Studio)
- Check "Audience Insights"
- Note: Peak days/times (e.g., "Monday-Friday 7pm")

**Method 2: Check Your Top Posts**
- Review your 20 most-liked posts
- Note the day/time each was posted
- Pattern will emerge (e.g., "Tuesday 7pm, Thursday 1pm")

**Method 3: Test & Track**
- Post at different times for 2 weeks
- Track which times get higher engagement
- Use that data for planning

### Common Peak Times by Niche

| Niche | Peak Days | Peak Times |
|-------|-----------|-----------|
| Personal Finance | Mon-Fri | 7pm, 9am, 12pm |
| Productivity | Mon-Fri | 8am, 6pm |
| Wellness | Tue-Thu | 6am, 7pm |
| Entertainment | Wed-Sun | 7pm, 9pm |
| Lifestyle | Tue-Fri | 1pm, 7pm |
| Business | Mon-Wed | 10am, 3pm |

**General rule**: Early morning (6-8am), lunch (12-1pm), evening (6-9pm)

## The 70/20/10 Content Mix

### 70% EDUCATIONAL (Core Value)
Purpose: Build authority, provide actionable value
Examples:
- How-to guides ("How to save $500/month")
- Explanations ("Why most people fail at budgeting")
- Tips & tricks ("5 quick budgeting wins")
- Mistakes to avoid ("The biggest money mistake")
- Data/stats ("90% of people do this wrong")

### 20% ENTERTAINING (Keep Them Engaged)
Purpose: Build connection, keep feed fun
Examples:
- Personal stories (transformation, wins, failures)
- Relatable humor (money memes, funny reels)
- Polls/Questions (what's your biggest struggle?)
- Behind-the-scenes (how you create content)
- Celebrations (milestones, community wins)

### 10% PROMOTIONAL (Build Your Business)
Purpose: Convert followers to customers/community
Examples:
- Product launch
- Course promotion
- Free resource offer
- Link to services
- Community event invitation

**Sample week with 3 posts**:
- Mon: Educational (how-to) = 2.3 posts/week
- Wed: Entertainment (personal story) = 0.67 posts/week
- Fri: Promotional (product/link) = 0.33 posts/week
= 70/20/10 mix ✓

## Batch Production Planning

### What is Batching?
Create 4+ weeks of content in 1-2 production days instead of daily

**Advantage**: 
- More efficient (get in flow state)
- Consistent quality (same day, same energy)
- Less decision fatigue
- Easier scheduling

### Batch Schedule Example

**RESEARCH DAY (2 hours)**
- Find 8 trending topics in niche
- Rank by viral potential
- Pick 8 topics for next 4 weeks

**PRODUCTION DAYS (2 days, 6 hours each)**
- Day 1: Create all hooks + scripts for 8 topics (6 hours)
- Day 2: Record voiceovers + edit videos (6 hours)
- Total: 12 hours of production for 8 videos (1.5 hours per video)

**AFTER PRODUCTION (1 hour)**
- Write captions + hashtags for all 8
- Schedule in advance (3-4 weeks ahead)

**Total time**: ~13 hours for 8 weeks of content (3x/week)
vs. daily creation: ~2 hours/week × 8 weeks = 16 hours

**Time saved**: ~3 hours per cycle

## Scheduling Tools

### Free Options:
- **Later.com** (free tier): Schedule 30 posts
- **Buffer.com** (free tier): Schedule + analytics
- **Meta Business Suite**: Free, built-in scheduling

### Paid Options:
- **Later Pro** ($15/mo): Unlimited posts + analytics
- **Hootsuite** ($50/mo): Multiple platforms + full analytics
- **Agorapulse** ($99/mo): Advanced features

**Process**:
1. Create content (videos, captions, hashtags)
2. Schedule in tool 3-4 weeks ahead
3. Tool auto-posts at optimal time
4. You focus on engagement (comments/replies) only

## Calendar Maintenance

### Weekly Review (5 minutes)
- Check scheduled posts for next week
- Fix any broken links/captions
- Respond to comments on live posts

### Monthly Review (30 minutes)
- Analyze top 10 posts: What worked?
- Note trending topics to capitalize on
- Adjust next month's calendar based on wins

### Quarterly Review (1 hour)
- Analyze all 12 weeks of content
- What type performed best? (Educational, Entertainment, Promotional)
- What topics drove engagement?
- Update strategy for next quarter

## Calendar Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Ran out of content ideas | Reference "8-week template" + trending research |
| Video delayed, post missing | Have 1-2 backup posts in reserve |
| Low engagement this week | Compare to analytics, note what worked |
| Audience size plateauing | Increase promotional posts (CTAs) or content frequency |
| Burnout from daily posting | Switch to 2-3x/week instead, batch better |
